Mohsin Khan

Dr. Khan holds a Ph.D. in Public Policy from Northeastern University and a Ph.D. in Sociology (Development Studies) from China Agricultural University. He brings more than fifteen years of experience in research, policy, and practice with governments, donors, and nonprofit organizations across Asia, Africa, and the United States, and is the founder of TransHelp, a semifinalist in the 2024 MIT Solve Global Health Equity Challenge.

Research

His research examines how political institutions, public investment, and climate change shape spatial inequality, public health, human mobility, and community resilience. Drawing on political economy, development economics, and environmental justice, his work combines GIS, remote sensing, spatial statistics, econometrics, machine learning, and participatory methods, with field research in Pakistan and the United States.

  • Urban Political Economy and Spatial InequalityInfrastructure, public investment, and access to services
  • Climate, Health, and Human MobilityFood security, floods, heat, and migration
  • Governance and Environmental JusticeEmergency policy, resilience, and urban greening
  • Participatory ResearchCommunity narratives and equity in planning
