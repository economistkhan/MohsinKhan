I have a Ph.D. in Public Policy from Northeastern University (Boston) and a Ph.D. in Sociology (Development Studies) from China Agricultural University. I have over fifteen years of experience in research, policy, and practice with governments, donors, and nonprofit organizations across Asia, Africa, and the United States, and also the founder of award-winning health-tech startup TransHelp, a semifinalist in the 2024 MIT Solve Global Health Equity Challenge.

Research

My research examines how political institutions, public investment, and climate change shape spatial inequality, public health, human mobility, and community resilience & vice versa. Drawing on Political economy, Development economics, Spatial and Environmental justice, my work combines GIS, remote sensing, spatial economics, machine learning, and participatory methods, with field research in Pakistan and the United States.
