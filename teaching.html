Teaching Assistant, Spring 2023. Prof. Ted Landsmark and Prof. Abdel Mustafa, Northeastern University

Diversity, equity, inclusion, and justice

As an internal migrant from the war affected region of South Waziristan in northwestern Pakistan, I grappled with language barriers and ethnic discrimination in Islamabad, the nation's capital. Most of the community spoke Urdu or Punjabi, while my native language, Pashto, affected my ability to learn and communicate with peers and teachers. I continued to excel as a student because of one teacher who helped me overcome these barriers. These experiences inspired me to devote myself to creating diverse, inclusive, and fair academic and professional environments where all individuals, irrespective of language, background, race, gender, sexual orientation, or religion, are respected, valued, and given equal opportunities.

In my research and fieldwork in Asia and Africa with donors and NGOs, I witnessed the effects of social and environmental inequality on vulnerable populations and collaborated with community organizations, governments, and academia on projects to empower marginalized groups. As founder of TransHelp, a trans led health technology startup, I have worked to democratize affordable health and legal aid for gender minorities in Pakistan. As a founder of the Youth Advocacy Council Pakistan, I led projects centered on gender and religious minorities, providing equal employment and leadership opportunities; its board of directors today is 50 percent minorities. I also took part in efforts that supported legislation by the National Assembly of Pakistan granting the transgender community the right to vote.

In my teaching in Pakistan, South Korea, and the United States, I strive to create an inclusive and supportive learning environment, encourage open dialogue, mutual respect, and appreciation of diverse viewpoints, and help students realize their potential. I remain committed to fostering an academic environment where all individuals are valued and empowered to thrive.
